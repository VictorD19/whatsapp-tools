## Recording sequence once eligible

1. Identify the pilot and release; sign into the service without filming secrets.
2. Show the minimum Meta authorization and connected username.
3. Capture the eligible inbound appearing in Conversas, including the event
   channel/timestamps in private diagnostic evidence. Polling/history does not
   prove webhook delivery.
4. Show a specifically authorized reply to the controlled account, inside 24h.
   Standby and other recipients must remain blocked. Do not enable AI replies.
5. Demonstrate sender cancellation and operator deletion separately. Confirm
   content disappears and is not restored by a duplicate event.
6. Show disconnect/data-removal instructions using an appropriate disposable
   connection. Keep the owner's ongoing test connection intact.
7. Inspect the final recording for secrets and verify persisted attachments by
   reopening the draft. Authorization-only video cannot substitute for this flow.
